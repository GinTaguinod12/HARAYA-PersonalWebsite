Who we are

About Our Team

We are a team of five students from National University Manila brought together by our shared passion for technology, creativity, and innovation. Each member brings a different perspective, skill set, and area of interest from developing mobile applications and websites to designing creative digital systems and exploring new technological ideas.

Rather than having the same strengths, we believe that our differences make our team stronger. We combine our individual talents, experiences, and creativity to turn ideas into meaningful digital projects.

This website serves as our central creative space, where you can discover the work and personality behind each member. Every member has their own personal page, following a consistent overall design while showcasing their unique skills, hobbies, interests, personality, and projects. Together, we are not just building websites and applications we are learning, creating, and turning ideas into digital experiences.
